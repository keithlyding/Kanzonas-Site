## About Kanzonas

Hi, I'm Keith Lyding. I make KanzonasPDF and KanzonasCAD.

**Why.** [CHECK] I work in industrial automation and controls, which means spending a lot of my
day with drawings: marking up PDFs of plans and schematics, and drawing electrical schematics
and panel layouts. The tools for that are expensive, tied to subscriptions and accounts, and
often more complicated than the job needs. AutoCAD Electrical in particular frustrated me
enough that I started building my own. KanzonasPDF came from the same itch: I wanted a PDF
editor that does markups and measuring well, without a subscription.

**What I promise.**
- Free, for good: no subscriptions, no trial that runs out, no paid feature tiers, no ads.
- No account, and nothing uploaded: your files stay on your computer.
- No telemetry. The apps only go online to check for updates (and KanzonasPDF for a
  signature's trusted timestamp, if you add one).
- Open source: anyone can read, check and build the code.

**How they're built.** I develop the apps with the help of AI coding assistants, and every
change goes through me before it's released. Every release is built by GitHub's own servers
straight from the public source code, and only after its automated tests and a self-test of
the finished program pass (more than 1,000 tests for KanzonasPDF). Bugs people report are
usually fixed within days.

**Why "Kanzonas"?** [CHECK] (A sentence about the name and the cactus and sunflower, if you'd
like to share it. Delete this paragraph otherwise.)

**Get in touch.** I'd love to hear what you use the apps for, what's missing, and what's
annoying. Write to support@kanzonas.com, or open an issue on GitHub
(KanzonasPDF / KanzonasCAD). Telling a colleague about the apps, or starring them on GitHub,
helps other people find them.
