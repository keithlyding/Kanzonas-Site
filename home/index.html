Free tools for people who work with drawings and documents

Open-source desktop apps for Windows and Linux, and KanzonasPDF for Mac too. No account, no subscription, no telemetry: your files stay on your computer.

KanzonasPDF

A PDF viewer and editor: comment, edit text, fill in and sign forms, redact and recognize text. For drawings and plans, takeoffs to scale, revision clouds and compare. For Windows, Linux and Mac.

Learn more and download →

KanzonasCAD

Electrical CAD for people who know AutoCAD but want fewer surprises. Schematics and panel layouts, wire numbers, cross-references that update themselves, and a bill of materials.

Learn more and download →

KanzonasSVG

Turns pictures (logos, drawings, scans) into SVG files, with exact colors and sharp edges. It picks the settings for each picture; you save the SVG or paste it into Inkscape. For Windows and Linux.
