Privacy

In short: no cookies, no tracking, no telemetry, and your files stay on your computer.

Last updated 7 November 2026

This website

  • No cookies, no ads, no tracking across sites, and no forms.
  • Visit counts: the site is hosted by Cloudflare and counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or identify you. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your IP address to deliver and protect the site (see Cloudflare's privacy policy).
  • Download buttons: on the app pages, your browser asks GitHub for the newest version, so GitHub sees that request (see GitHub's privacy statement). The downloads themselves come from GitHub too.
  • AlternativeTo badge: on the app pages, the "Listed on AlternativeTo" picture is loaded from alternativeto.net.

The apps

  • Your files never leave your computer. There's no account, no cloud and no telemetry.
  • KanzonasPDF only goes online to check whether a newer version exists (you can turn that off) and, if you add a trusted timestamp to a digital signature, to reach a timestamp server. Recognizing text (OCR) works offline. Scanning talks to scanners on your own network.
  • KanzonasCAD only goes online to check for and download new versions.

Email and bug reports

  • Mail to support@kanzonas.com is forwarded by Cloudflare to Keith's inbox and used only to answer you.
  • Bug reports on GitHub are public, so please don't put private details or confidential files in them.

Questions

Write to support@kanzonas.com.
