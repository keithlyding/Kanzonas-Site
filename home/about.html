About Kanzonas

I'm Keith Lyding. KanzonasPDF, KanzonasCAD and KanzonasSVG are my apps.

Why

I have 20 years in the industrial electrical/controls field, which means spending a lot of my day with drawings: marking up PDFs of plans and schematics, and drawing electrical schematics and panel layouts. The tools for that are expensive, tied to subscriptions and accounts, and often more complicated than the job needs. AutoCAD Electrical in particular frustrated me. With how advanced AI had gotten, I decided to see if it could write some apps for me. Not only were they adequate, but they did everything I needed and more. Since they were so helpful to me, I figured that others could benefit from them as well.

Important notes about the apps

  • No account, and nothing uploaded: your files stay on your computer.
  • No telemetry. The apps only go online to check for updates.
  • Open source: anyone can read, check and build the code.

How they're built

I develop the apps with the help of AI coding assistants, and every change goes through me before it's released. Every release is built by GitHub's own servers straight from the public source code, and only after its automated tests and a self-test of the finished program pass. Bugs people report are usually fixed within days.

Making sure it isn't AI slop

AI writes most of the code, so the checks have to be strict, and they're done by machines on every build, not left to good intentions:

  • Nothing is released untested. Each release is published only after all its automated tests pass, and a self-test of the finished program, on every system it's made for. KanzonasPDF alone has more than 1,000 tests.
  • Batteries that try to break the apps. In KanzonasPDF and KanzonasCAD, thousands of random edits, as a person might make them, with undo, redo and saving mixed in, checking after every step that nothing was lost or changed by accident. KanzonasSVG gets odd and broken picture files and thousands of random actions in its window, and its tracing engine runs under memory and thread checkers and a fuzzer.
  • A slop check on every build. It looks for what careless or machine-written code leaves behind: leftover TODO notes and placeholders, debugging code, code nothing uses, errors silently ignored, copied code, tests that check nothing, filler words like "seamless" and "robust", and help text that names a command or file that doesn't exist. What it finds is fixed before a release.
  • Speed limits. Each build times real work, like opening a drawing of 60,000 lines, and fails if the app has gotten slower than its limit.
  • Tests are never weakened to pass. A failing test gets its cause fixed, not the test changed: that's a written rule for every AI agent that works on the code.
  • Weekly security checks of the libraries KanzonasPDF and KanzonasCAD use to read files.
  • I use the apps for my own work, and every change goes through me before it's released.

Why "Kanzonas"?

Kanzonas is really just a merge of Kansas and Arizona. My wife is from Kansas, and I'm from Arizona.
