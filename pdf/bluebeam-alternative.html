The Bluebeam-style tools, free

Scale and measure

Set the scale from a preset (1/4" = 1'-0", 1:100...), by calibrating a known dimension, or from the title block. Length, polylength, area, perimeter, volume, angle, radius and counts, with snapping and a summary you can export to CSV.

Spaces, room by room

Draw named spaces (Room 101) and the markups list shows which space each markup is in and filters by it: takeoffs and punch lists room by room.

Markups that mean something

Revision clouds, callouts, stamps with your name and the date, numbered dots, and a tool chest of saved styles. Legends that list and total the markups on a sheet.

Markups list

Every markup in one list you can filter (by layer, by space and more) and export. Groups and layers, hidden in other PDF programs too.

Compare and replace

Compare two revisions of a sheet with every change clouded. Replace a sheet with its new revision and keep your markups.

Sets and split view

Browse a folder of sheets as one set by sheet number and title, and see two places side by side with synced pan and zoom. Rulers along the page.

Where KanzonasPDF is different

Honestly, so you know before you switch:

  • No cloud: no live shared sessions or online project storage. Share PDFs the way you already do (email, a shared drive, your document system).
  • No iPad or phone app, and on a Mac only Apple silicon (M1 or newer).
  • A young project: try it on a copy of a real set before you rely on it, and tell us what's missing. Most of its features came from requests.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes: free and open source (AGPL), with no watermarks, no trial period and no paid version. No account, and nothing is uploaded.

Will other programs see my markups?

Yes. Markups are saved as standard PDF annotations, so Acrobat and other PDF programs show them too.

Can it handle big drawings?

That's what it's built for. Every build checks that a sheet of 60,000 lines opens and draws in under 5 seconds, and that zooming stays fast.

Does it go online?

Only to check whether a newer version exists (you can turn that off), and to reach a timestamp server if you add a trusted timestamp to a digital signature.
