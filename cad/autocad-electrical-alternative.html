What you'd use AutoCAD Electrical for, free

Works like AutoCAD

Command line, object snaps, ortho, polar tracking, grips, and window, crossing and lasso selection. If you know AutoCAD, you already know how to draw here.

Wire numbers

Wire numbers that start with the page number (6001) and number themselves, with distribution blocks (6001A) and off-sheet connectors. Wires land exactly on pins and go around the symbols in their way, and their layers carry the gauge and color.

Cross-references

Device tags by page number (CR601), contacts linked to their coil, and grid-style cross-references (6-D1) that update themselves.

JIC/NFPA symbols

Schematic symbols and panel footprints built in, with parts by manufacturer and catalog number and ratings that go on the bill of materials. Three-phase power, fused disconnects and starters, terminal strips and PLC I/O points.

Panel layouts

Panel sheets at a scale, with DIN rail and wire duct at real size. Footprints snap onto the rail and carry their schematic device's tag. Print the set, or save it as PDF.

Your AutoCAD Electrical drawings

Open DWG (AutoCAD R13 to 2018 format) and DXF. Blocks stay blocks, and AutoCAD Electrical's attributes (P_TAG1, DESC1, MFG, CAT) become tags and parts.

Where KanzonasCAD is different

Honestly, so you know before you switch:

  • US practice only: JIC/NFPA symbols, US device tags, drawings in inches on ANSI sheets. No IEC symbol library.
  • Reads DWG but saves DXF and PDF, not DWG.
  • Not there yet: wire lists and terminal strip reports, check-out and check-in of sheets for teams, and shared libraries. They're next on the roadmap.
  • A young project (version 0.x): try it on a real job alongside your current tools first, and tell us what's missing.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes: free and open source (MIT), with no subscription, no account and no paid version.

Who makes it?

Keith Lyding, who has worked in industrial electrical and controls for 20 years. AutoCAD Electrical frustrated him, so he had AI write the electrical CAD program he wanted; it did everything he needed, so he shares it.

Can I bring in my title block?

Yes: an imported title block can become the project's, and the title block fills itself in (drawn date, print stamp, revision history).

Does it go online?

Only to check for and download new versions.
